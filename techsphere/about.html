About TechSphere

Technology, explained clearly and practically.

TechSphere is an independent educational technology publication focused on making modern technology easier to understand. We publish practical tutorials, explainers and guides about artificial intelligence, Python, developer tools and software engineering.

Our editorial approach

Our goal is to publish content that is useful, understandable and technically responsible. Tutorials should explain not only what to do, but why a technique works and where its limitations are.

Accuracy and updates

Technology changes quickly. We aim to review content when tools or recommended practices change. Readers should check official documentation for version-specific behavior and production requirements.
